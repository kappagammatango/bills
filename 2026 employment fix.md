*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of Firestone in Congress assembled,*

**SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This bill may be known as the "Better Employment Act of 2026".

**SEC. 2. EFFECTIVENESS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This Act shall be effective immediately.

**SEC. 3. SEVERABILITY**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Should any part of this Act be struck down or declared unenforceable, then that part of the Act shall be considered severed from this Act, and the rest shall remain in full force.

**SEC. 4. REPEAL**

The Employment Act of 2026 shall be repealed.

**SEC. 5. DEFINITIONS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) As used in this Act, "command position" means an agency head or deputy head, and includes the Chief of Staff to the Governor.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (b) As used in this Act, "supervisory position" means any position other than a command position which entails direct or indirect supervision of other employees of the agency. A court shall, absent clear and convincing evidence to the contrary, give deference to the determinations of an agency head in what positions are supervisory.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (c) As used in this Act, "agency" does not include the executive offices of—

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (i) the Governor;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (iii) Stapleton County; or

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) a municipality.

**SEC. 6. LIMITS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) An individual may hold no more than 1 command position in any agency of the State of Firestone or its inferior governments.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (1) An individual holding a command position in any agency may not serve in the legislature of the same level of government.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (2) An individual holding a command position in a state agency may not serve in the judiciary.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (b) An individual may hold no more than 2 supervisory positions in any agency of the State of Firestone or its inferior governments.

(d)(1) The Governor and Lieutenant Governor may not be employed in any agency of the State of Firestone.

(2) The Governor and Lieutenant Governor may not serve as a legislator, or in the judiciary.

(e) An individual serving in the judiciary may not serve as a legislator.

(f)(1) The County Executive may not be employed in any agency of Stapleton County.

(2) The County Executive may not serve as a legislator.

(g) The Mayor and Deputy Mayor of a municipality may not be employed in any agency of that municipality.

(h) No individual may simultaneously be Governor, Lieutenant Governor, County Executive, Mayor, or Deputy Mayor.

(i) There shall be no limit on the number of positions an individual may hold, if in compliance with the preceding sections.

**SEC. 7. ENFORCEMENT**

(a) Should an individual be in violation of this Act as a result of entering a position, such individual shall make themself legally compliant with this Act within 7 days of receiving notice of their violation, or they shall be considered to have vacated positions which made them non-compliant, in chronological order.

(b) No individual who holds the rank of "Cadet", "Candidate", "Trainee" or any other reasonable variant of such rank in a department shall be considered employed in that department.

(c) Any rank of an individual within a Firestone group, discord server, or another reasonable variant shall not be rendered legally binding unless such individual was lawfully or constitutionally appointed, elected, or employed in such rank as defined by applicable law.

(d) The Department of Justice may bring a cause of action against any individual in violation of this Act, who may be ordered to comply with this Act by a court.
