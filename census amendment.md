**A BILL TO FIX THE CENSUS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; *Be it enacted by the State of Firestone Congress here assembled,*

**§ 1. TITLE**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This Act may be known as "An Act to Make the Census Better”.

**§ 2. ENACTMENT INTO LAW**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This Act shall go into effect upon the completion of the constitutionally required processes.

**§ 3. SEVERABILITY**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Should any part of this Legislation be struck down and/or declared unenforceable, then that part shall be severed with the rest remaining in full force and effect unless all is struck down and/or declared unenforceable.

**§ 4. AMENDMENTS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Section 5(a) of The Census Act of 2022 currently states: "The census shall include the following questions that pertain to the following subjects, but shall not be limited to: Estimated length of citizenship in Firestone, nationality, ethnicity, gender, income, and current occupation(s) in Firestone."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Section 5(a) of The Census Act of 2022 shall now state: "The census shall include the following questions that pertain to the following subjects, but shall not be limited to: Estimated length of citizenship in Firestone, nationality, race, gender, income, and current occupation(s) in Firestone."

*Respectfully submitted to the Congress of the State of Firestone*
