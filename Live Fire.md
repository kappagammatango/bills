<h1><b>Law Enforcement Training Exemption Act</b></h1>

 
</div>

<br/>
 
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of Firestone in Congress assembled,*

---

**SECTION 1 - SEVERABILITY & ENACTMENT**

(a) Should any part of this act be struck down and/or declared unenforceable, then that part shall be severed with the rest remaining in full force and effect unless all is struck down and/or declared unenforceable.

(b) This act shall enter into effect immediately after its passage by the constitutionally required processes.

**SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS**

(a) "Live Fire Training" shall be defined as any training that utilizes live ammunition and is likely to cause a decrease in health of a player. 

**SECTION 3 - IMMUNITY**

(a) Any State of Firestone, County, or Municipal law enforcement department officially established by law may conduct live-fire trainings in Stapleton County V2. Employees of the participating department(s) and any assisting civilians shall not be arrested, prosecuted, sued, or disciplined for actions that were conducted during the training, that were against participating employees/civilians, and that were reasonably necessary to simulate a realistic scenario and response. 

(b) Live Fire Trainings start date/times and end date/times shall be documented by each department. All live fire trainings shall have a marked perimeter line that signifies the training area if there are non-participants in the server being utilized. Departments shall ensure that non-participants are not within the the training area and shall be authorized to remove non-participants from the training area. Departments shall conduct the trainings in a server with less than 5 non-participants and shall make a reasonable effort to have the server closed to access by non-participants. The department shall not conduct a training in an area that is occupied, or could potentially be occupied (such as spawnpoints), by non-participants if they are present in the server, or if the server is not closed to non-participants;
