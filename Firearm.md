# **WEAPONS TRAFFICKING ACT**

<div align="left">

<hr>

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of Firestone in Congress assembled,*

<div align="left">

### SECTION I: TITLE

(a) This Act shall be known as the “Weapons Trafficking Act.”

### SECTION II: DEFINITIONS

(a) The Firestone Criminal Code shall be defined as [here.](https://trello.com/b/EGN3OQzQ/firestone-criminal-code)

### SECTION III: ENFORCEMENT

(a) This Act shall be enforced by any relevant parties.

### SECTION IV: WEAPONS TRAFFICKING

(A) Chapter 5, Section 7 of the Firestone Criminal Code shall be established and be titled,
“§7 – Weapons Trafficking.” It shall be recognized as a Class C Felony carrying a jail time of 600 seconds. It shall state:

"(a) The act of weapons trafficking shall be recognized as an unlawful criminal offense within the State of Firestone.

(b) Weapons trafficking shall be defined as operating a vehicle in the State of Firestone designated for the transportation of weapons or ammunition, except as may be authorized by law.

(c) Law enforcement and National Guard personnel acting within their official duties or investigative operations shall be exempt from this Section."

### SECTION V: SEVERABILITY AND ENACTMENT

(a) Should any part of this bill be struck down and/or declared unenforceable, then that part shall be severed with the rest remaining in full force and effect unless all is struck down and/or declared unenforceable.

(b) This bill shall be entered into effect as an Act of Congress immediately after completing the constitutionally required process.
