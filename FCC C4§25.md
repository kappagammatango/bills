**A BILL TO PROTECT PUBLIC EMPLOYEES FROM FRAUD**

Proposed by: *Senator wierdo25*

</div>

---

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; *Be it enacted by the State of Firestone Congress here assembled,*

**§ 1. TITLE**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This Act shall be known as the "A Bill to Protect Public Employees from Fraud".

<br/>

**§ 2. ENACTMENT INTO LAW**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) This Act shall go into effect upon the completion of the constitutionally required processes.

<br/>

**§ 3. SEVERABILITY**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Should any part of this Legislation be struck down and/or declared unenforceable, then that part shall be severed with the rest remaining in full force and effect unless all is struck down and/or declared unenforceable.

<br/>

**§ 4. DEFINITIONS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) For the purposes of this Bill, the 'Firestone Criminal Code', as described by the [Criminal Code Revision Act (R)](https://forums.stateoffirestone.com/t/12405), shall be defined as: <https://trello.com/b/EGN3OQzQ>

<br/>

**§ 5. AMENDMENTS**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (a) Chapter 4 of the Firestone Criminal Code shall be amended.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (i) Chapter 4 Section 25 Subsection c of the Firestone Criminal Code currently states: "Commissioned law enforcement officers; voluntary peace officers, including (but not limited to) non-certified correctional officers; or national guardsmen while acting in their official capacity when on-duty shall be exempt from this Section."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; (ii) Chapter 4 Section 25 Subsection c of the Firestone Criminal Code shall now state: "Commissioned law enforcement officers; voluntary peace officers, including (but not limited to) non-certified correctional officers; **national guardsmen; or public service employees** while acting in their official capacity when on-duty shall be exempt from this Section."

<br/>
